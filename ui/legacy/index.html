How it works

One transaction, one trust boundary

The transaction layer owns the decision. AI services only return signals. Nothing slow sits on the decision path, and every model runs inside IBM Z.

  1. Upload. The photo is decoded, cropped, hashed and encrypted at upload time, so the decision path only runs inference.
  2. Submit. The claim and its evidence ID go to transaction CLMI. Personal data is pseudonymised before anything is stored or scored.
  3. One internal call. The risk service runs privacy rules, the detector and the metadata/duplicate checks in parallel, under a hard 300 ms deadline.
  4. Signals back. Calibrated AI score, duplicate match, metadata flags and timings.
  5. Decide and commit. The COBOL rule CLMIPOL sets the band. The claim, decision and journal entry commit in one transaction, and the band is returned.
  6. Explain, after the fact. For AMBER and RED only, Granite on LinuxONE writes an advisory note. It can't change the band.
Decision policy

Three bands, set by a false-positive cap

Thresholds are fitted on LinuxONE scores so that genuine photos are flagged RED at most 1% of the time, and AMBER at most 5%.

RED

Calibrated score ≥ τred, or the same photo was already used on a different claim.

Referred to an investigator with the reasons. Never auto-rejected.

AMBER

τamber ≤ score < τred, or metadata names an editing or generation tool, or the risk service is unavailable.

Human review. The fail-safe: an error is never an approval.

GREEN

None of the above.

Fast-tracked to normal processing.

Built on IBM Z

Why LinuxONE

Data never leaves

Original photos and personal data stay inside IBM Z. Every model runs inside the boundary, including the LLM.

AI next to the transaction

The detector is compiled with the IBM Z Deep Learning Compiler, targets the Telum on-chip AI accelerator (NNPA) where available, and answers within the claims transaction.

COBOL where it belongs

The decision rule is real COBOL. In production, the CICS transaction on z/OS reaches LinuxONE over HiperSockets, memory to memory.

Responsible AI

Designed so honest people aren't harmed

  • A person makes every adverse decision. RED means investigation, in line with UK GDPR Article 22.
  • False positives are capped and reported per subgroup, so people with older phones or low-light photos aren't flagged more often.
  • Bands and reasons, never a bare "FAKE". The detector score is one signal, not proof.
  • The LLM is advisory, runs after the decision, and treats claim text as untrusted input.
  • Pseudonymised by default. Only an authorised role can re-link identity, and every re-link is journaled.
  • Egress allowlist + log for anything that ever leaves.
