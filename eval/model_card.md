# Model card: AI-edited claim photo detector

*Model `det-r18-v4`, thresholds `t-0` (preliminary, Modal FP32). Thresholds move to `t-1` after the LinuxONE parity check.*

## Intended use

- **Task.** Score one motor-insurance claim photo for signs that it was generated or edited by an image-generation model. Output is a calibrated probability, `ai_score`.
- **Where it runs.** Inside the claims transaction on IBM LinuxONE, compiled with the IBM Z Deep Learning Compiler. Original photos never leave IBM Z.
- **How it is used.** One signal among several (duplicate-photo check, metadata flags). The transaction layer maps scores to bands with versioned thresholds. **RED means a person investigates; nothing is rejected automatically.**
- **Out of scope.** Proving fraud; judging people; video; documents; photos of anything other than vehicles; any decision without human review.

## Model

- ResNet-18 (torchvision ImageNet weights), fine-tuned end to end, single logit. 15 epochs, AdamW (1e-4 backbone, 1e-3 head), batch 64, balanced sampler; augmentation: colour jitter, realistic degradation (downscale to 35–100%, blur, JPEG 30–95) for both classes, and crops centred on the edit for 80% of inflated fakes.
- Input: three 224×224 crops (centre plus two grid cells) after shrinking the short side to 640 px (never enlarging smaller photos). Logits are averaged, divided by a temperature fitted on validation, and passed through a sigmoid. Full recipe in `train/HANDOFF.md`.
- Alternative candidate: frozen CLIP ViT-B/16 image encoder plus logistic regression (UnivFD approach), evaluated on the same matrix.

## Training data

See `data/dataset_card.md` for counts, licences and filters, and `data/spotcheck_log.md` for the visual checks.

- **Real:** CarDD (4,000 damage photos, research licence), Wikimedia Commons crash and damage photos uploaded before 2022 (CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, attribution kept).
- **Fake, all generated by us on Modal:**
  - Regenerated copies of every real photo (Stable Diffusion 2 inpainting with an empty mask, B-Free's self-conditioned fakes), so real and fake share content.
  - Damage inflation: a real photo with a small inpainted damage patch pasted back.
  - Fully fabricated scenes from SDXL base 1.0.
  - FLUX.1-schnell images, **test only**, as an unseen generator.
- Split 70/15/15 by group (a real photo and all its fakes stay together).

## Controls against learning unrelated patterns

| Risk | Control | Check |
|---|---|---|
| "Damage = fake" | Real pile is mostly damage; damage measured by CLIP, not prompts; undamaged fakes capped at the real undamaged share | Damage-share gap in dataset card |
| Content or scene type | Every real photo has a regenerated twin with the same content | `regenerated_only` evaluation row |
| Image size, aspect, resampling | Generated scenes cropped to real photo sizes without upscaling; every image gets the same random resize | Dimension shortcut AUROC ≈ 0.5 |
| Compression history | Camera-JPEG pass for generated pixels only; same final JPEG quality range for all | File-size shortcut AUROC ≈ 0.5 |
| Global colour | Colour jitter for both classes in training | Colour shortcut AUROC, content-matched ≈ 0.5 |
| Monochrome or historic photos only in the real pile | Monochrome and single-tint Commons photos removed | Spot-check log |
| Watermarks and plates | Regenerated twins reproduce them, so they appear in both classes | Heatmap check on demo cases |

## Evaluation

Chosen model `det-r18-v4`. Full tables, candidate comparison and audits in `eval/results.md`. Test split, Modal FP32, thresholds `t-0`.

| Test set | AUROC | TPR at 1% FPR | Real FPR at τ_red |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-distribution | 0.936 | 0.674 | 1.2% |
| FLUX.1-schnell, never trained on | 0.944 | 0.502 | 1.2% |
| Real vs its own regenerated copy | 0.985 | 0.746 | 1.2% |
| Damage inflation (partial edits) | 0.622 | 0.041 | 1.2% |
| Real damage photos | — | — | 1.2% |
| JPEG quality 50 | 0.908 | 0.402 | 2.3% |
| Half-size photos | 0.807 | 0.183 | 16.8% |

Real-photo RED rate by subgroup (JPEG quality, size, compressibility, source, damage): 0.4% to 2.8%. LinuxONE parity: `eval/parity_t-1.md` once B has run the compiled model.

## Thresholds

- `τ_red`: score at which the real-photo false-positive rate on validation is at most 1%.
- `τ_amber`: the same at 5%.
- Fitted on the scores the compiled model produces **on LinuxONE**, because NNPA's 16-bit arithmetic shifts scores slightly. Versioned config `eval/thresholds_t-<n>.json`.

## Known limitations and failure modes

- **New generators.** Detection drops on generators not seen in training. The FLUX row measures this, and it is reported rather than hidden.
- **Heavy compression and resizing.** Messaging apps and screenshots weaken generation traces. The compression rows measure this. Small photos are the worst case: 16.8% of half-size real test photos exceed τ_red (the first model flagged 89%; degradation augmentation and never upscaling brought it down). Recommended policy: below 480 px on the short side, the AI score alone can give AMBER but not RED.
- **Partial edits.** A small inpainted patch is caught far less often than a fully generated image (AUROC 0.62). The model does see edits when a crop covers them (AUROC 0.84), so the limit is crop coverage. The heatmap shows adjusters where to look.
- **Residual dependence on photo cleanliness.** Among real photos, smoother and more compressible ones score slightly higher (Spearman −0.22 with bytes per pixel), partly because generated images lack sensor noise. RED rates stay within 0.4–2.8% across subgroups.
- **Adaptive fraudsters.** Someone who knows the detector can try to evade it, for example by re-photographing a screen. The duplicate-photo check and human review are the backstop.
- **Camera and phone diversity.** Real training photos come from web sources. FPR on new cameras is measured on the team's held-out phone photos when available.

## Ethical considerations

- No automated adverse decision, consistent with UK GDPR Article 22: RED routes to a person, with reasons.
- False-positive rates are capped by threshold and reported per real-photo source, so groups of honest claimants are not flagged more often.
- No photos showing injured people are used: Commons rescue scenes are removed by a scene filter, and the spot-check confirms it.
- Images are not redistributed; only manifests and attribution are published.

## Retraining plan

Add newly released generators to the fake pile as they appear; refresh thresholds on LinuxONE scores after every retrain; keep FLUX-style held-out generators for honest generalisation numbers; add the team phone photos and real claim photos (with consent) to the real pile.
